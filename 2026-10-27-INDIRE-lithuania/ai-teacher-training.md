# Background and Rationale

- AI creates opportunities and challenges for education.
- Teachers need the knowledge, skills, and confidence to use AI effectively and responsibly.
- Evidence is needed on whether structured training changes classroom practices and student experiences.

**Overall objective:** Evaluate the impact of AI-focused teacher training to inform education policy and practice in Italy and Europe.

# Research Objectives and Questions

## Two levels of impact

- **Teachers:** Knowledge, attitudes, confidence, competencies, and professional practices.
- **Students:** Learning experiences, engagement, and perceptions of AI-supported teaching.

**Key research question:** To what extent does structured AI teacher training influence teaching practices and student outcomes, and how do effects vary across school contexts?

# Sampling and Recruitment

- Starting population: approximately 8,000 public schools.
- Target sample: approximately 1,000 participating schools.
- Stratified sampling across geographic regions and socioeconomic contexts.
- Participating schools formally agree to follow the study protocol.

*Target sample is subject to recruitment and participation.*

# Baseline Assessment

**Before training begins**

- Schools submit lists of enrolled teachers.
- Schools commit to administering the student questionnaire.
- Teachers complete a baseline self-reflection assessment.

**Baseline measures**

- AI knowledge and competencies
- Attitudes and confidence
- Existing AI-related teaching practices

# Teacher Training Intervention

- **11 modules**
- **12 hours total**
- **Asynchronous online course**

Co-developed by JRC and INDIRE, drawing on JRC research and wider academic literature.

**Three core themes**

- Pedagogical uses of AI
- Ethical and responsible AI adoption
- Practical applications of AI in education

# Immediate Post-training Evaluation

- Evaluate skills learned.
- Complete a final self-reflection assessment.
- Compare pre- and post-training measures.
- Examine changes in competencies, confidence, and intended teaching practices.

# Six-month Follow-up

**Teacher questionnaires**

- Adoption of AI-related classroom practices
- Perceived effects on teaching

**Student questionnaires**

- Learning experiences and processes
- Student engagement and perceptions

**Purpose:** Examine whether training translates into sustained classroom practices and perceived changes in teaching and learning.

# Quantitative Analysis

1. Compare pre- and post-training measures.
2. Examine six-month follow-up outcomes.
3. Explore differences by geographic region, school context, and socioeconomic background.

Student outcomes will be examined through questionnaire-based evidence on experiences and perceptions.

# Outputs and Policy Impact

**Main output:** A comprehensive report presenting empirical findings and evidence-based recommendations.

**Target audiences**

- Italian education policymakers and stakeholders
- European Commission and EU-level stakeholders
- Education leaders and teacher-training organisations

**Expected policy contribution**

- Inform teacher professional development
- Support evidence-informed AI policy
- Guide responsible educational innovation

- Recruitment and baseline assessment
- 12-hour teacher-training intervention
- Immediate post-training evaluation
- Six-month teacher and student follow-up
- Quantitative analysis and policy recommendations
